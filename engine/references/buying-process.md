# Buying process, title and legal protection

Verified 18–20 Nov 2026. Sources and quotes: `research/A-costs-legal.md` in the data folder. Confidence: **H** primary text, **M** good secondary, **L** commercial/unverified.

Contents: 1 Sequence and timing · 2 Title · 3 The notary · 4 Preliminary contract and deposit · 5 Documents · 6 Paying · 7 Agents · 8 Off-plan · 9 Foreign buyers · 10 What to ask a professional

## 1. Sequence and realistic timing

1. Screen remotely (title, off-plan, location, surface, syndic, building rules).
2. View.
3. Gather sold-price and rental evidence; settle financing.
4. Offer, in writing, subject to title, documents and (if relevant) the loan.
5. Your notary pulls a fresh ownership certificate and checks the documents.
6. Notarised preliminary contract (*compromis / promesse de vente*); deposit to the notary's account. Usually runs 1–3 months.
7. If borrowing: the bank's written offer, then a mandatory 10-day wait before you can accept.
8. Final deed (*acte de vente*); balance paid through the notary.
9. Notary registers with the tax office (within 30 days) and the land registry. **You own it when the registry records your name** — typically 2–3 weeks after signing. **M**

Agreed price to name on title: **3–6 weeks cash, 6–12 weeks with a Moroccan mortgage**; add 3–4 weeks if signing by power of attorney from abroad (a foreign power of attorney needs recognition by a Moroccan court). **M**

## 2. Title — the first question, always

| Status | Meaning | Position |
|---|---|---|
| **Titre foncier** (*titré*) | Registered at the land registry (ANCFCC). Entries prove ownership against the world. | What you want. **H** |
| **Réquisition** (*en cours d'immatriculation*) | Registration applied for; third parties can still object until the title issues. | Wait for the title, or price in real risk with a lawyer. **H** |
| **Melkia** (unregistered, *adoul* deeds) | Ownership rests on possession and traditional deeds. A sale deed does not itself establish ownership. Notaries do not draw deeds on it. Banks will not lend. | Walk away unless an independent lawyer clears it. The video agrees. **H** |

**The ownership certificate** (*certificat de propriété*): 100 MAD, same day, orderable online at ancfcc.gov.ma with the registry office and title number. The form asks for the applicant's identity, not proof of ownership — so a buyer can get one. It shows owner(s) and shares, surface, mortgages, seizures, provisional claims (*prénotation*), easements. Want one under a month old for the preliminary contract and re-pulled on completion day. **H / M**

Read it for:
- **Owner = seller**, and every co-owner signing.
- **Hypothèque** (mortgage): must be released at completion (500 MAD + notary).
- **Saisie** (seizure) or **prénotation**: stop until lifted.
- **Date the seller was registered**: a defrauded true owner can challenge an entry for **four years**. A seller registered recently deserves a harder look at how they acquired. **H**
- **Surface**: against the advert.
- **Indivision**: buying a *share* exposes you to co-owners' pre-emption (*chefaa*) for up to a year after registration. A separate apartment title is not affected. **H**

After completion, give the registry a working mobile and email so the **Mohafadati** alert service notifies you of any entry on your title. **M**

## 3. The notary

- A regulated legal profession (Law 32-09): entry exam, training, appointment by decree, compulsory insurance, statutory duty to verify and advise. **H**
- **Neutral**, advising both parties. Not your advocate. **H**
- **One notary** normally handles the sale; **the buyer chooses** because the buyer pays. On new builds the developer will steer you to theirs — you may still appoint your own. **M**
- **Client money must go to the notary's account at the Caisse de Dépôt et de Gestion (CDG)**, a state institution, and is released to the seller only after formalities and repayment of any mortgage. **M-H**
- A guarantee fund exists for notary default, but pays only after a court judgment. **M**
- **Check them**: Ordre des notaires directory (notaires.org.ma/annuaire), a physical office, reviews. The video's advice here is right.
- Since July 2026 a lawyer can no longer draw the sale deed, but can still be hired purely as *your* adviser — worth it for off-plan, anything not cleanly titled, or when the notary came from the other side. **M**

## 4. Preliminary contract and deposit

- **Must be an authentic (notarised) deed** since 16 July 2026 (Law 41-25); informal promises are void. **M**
- Deposit: no legal amount; **10% is custom**. Pay it to the **notary's CDG account**, never to the seller or agent. **M**
- If the *buyer* defaults, the seller keeps it; on mutual cancellation it is returned (civil code arts 288–290). No automatic "double" penalty on a defaulting seller — write one in. **H**
- **Conditions that make the deposit refundable must be written into the contract**:
  - loan refused by a stated date (the consumer law implies at least one month — ask for 60 days);
  - clean ownership certificate at completion;
  - tax and syndic clearances and certificate of occupancy delivered;
  - inventory of included furniture attached;
  - a matching penalty if the seller withdraws.

## 5. Documents to have before any money moves

| Document | Why |
|---|---|
| Fresh ownership certificate | Section 2. **H** |
| Seller's ID; notarised power of attorney if represented | Forged or stale powers of attorney are the classic fraud; a national register of property powers of attorney started in June 2026. **M** |
| **Tax clearance** (*quitus / attestation fiscale*) | The notary may not complete without it; unpaid taxes attach to the property. **H** |
| **Syndic statement** that charges are paid | The buyer is jointly liable for the seller's arrears (Law 18-00 art. 42). **H** |
| **Building rules** (*règlement de copropriété*) and last owners'-meeting minutes | Use restrictions (short lets), planned works, level of charges. |
| **Certificate of occupancy / conformity** (*permis d'habiter*) | Proof of lawful completion. **M** |
| Floor plan with surface; utility bills | Check against the title. |

## 6. Paying

- **Declare the whole price in the deed. Pay by bank transfer through the notary.**
- Under-declaration: side agreements are void (CGI art. 142); the tax office can reassess for four years with penalties up to 100% (arts 186, 232); the State can pre-empt within six months at the declared price + 5% (arts 143, 218). **H**
- Since 1 July 2026 any untraceable part of a price above 300,000 MAD costs the buyer an extra **2%** duty (art. 133-III). **H**
- Money paid outside the deed cannot be repatriated and shrinks your documented cost base.
- A seller who insists on cash "to reduce tax" is asking you to carry their risk. Decline; it is a reason to walk.

## 7. Agents

- **No licensing law** (a bill has been pending since 2014). Anyone can act as an intermediary. **H**
- Commission is contractual: commonly 2.5% including VAT, quoted range 2.5–5%; sometimes charged to both sides. **L**
- Before viewing, agree in writing: the rate, who pays, payable only on completion. Do not sign a viewing slip with open terms. Never give an agent a deposit.

## 8. Off-plan (VEFA, Law 44-00 as amended by 107-12)

What the statute says (civil code arts 618-1 to 618-20) **H**:
- No reservation or preliminary contract before the **building permit**; payments taken before a contract are void.
- Reservation: optional, one-month cooling-off, valid six months.
- **Maximum stage payments**: 5% at reservation, 5% at the preliminary contract (10% if no reservation), 10% at start of works, 60% across three construction phases, **20% at keys and final deed**.
- Developer must provide a **completion or refund guarantee** after the preliminary contract.
- Late payment by the buyer: at most 1% a month, 10% a year. Late delivery by the developer: 1% a month, 10% a year.
- Final deed only after the certificate of occupancy.

What happens in practice **M**:
- Most developers sell on their own reservation terms outside this framework; the guarantee's implementing decrees were still missing in 2023 (2026 status unverified); recovery through the courts takes years.
- So a schedule like the video's "20% + 20% + 30% + 30%" signals you are outside the statutory protection.

Checks: land title in the developer's name (pull the certificate yourself); permit number and date; a copy of the guarantee naming your unit; payments only to the project account or the notary; completed earlier projects you can visit; your own notary.

Whether an ordinary mortgage is available before the individual title exists is unverified — assume not, as the video says.

## 9. Foreign buyers

- **City apartments and houses**: no nationality restriction; same duties and taxes as Moroccans. **H**
- **Agricultural land outside the urban perimeter**: closed to foreigners unless a non-agricultural-use certificate (AVNA) is obtained for a real project; refused in high-value farming zones. Notaries must refuse unauthorised deeds. **M-H**
- A unit in an authorised subdivision outside the perimeter gets the AVNA as a matter of course. Check the perimeter for any "villa" or resort property.
- Moroccans living abroad are not affected.
- **Mortgage**: a foreigner must declare they own no other residence in Morocco (section on financing in `money.md`).
- **Exchange control**: see `money.md` — document the foreign-currency origin of everything.

## 10. Questions for the professionals

For the notary: Is any fee decree in force, or is your fee negotiable? Do the funds go through your CDG account? What exactly do you check before the preliminary contract? Which professionals can now draw an authentic deed? Can I sign by power of attorney, and what does it need?

For a lawyer (off-plan or anything unusual): Is this sale inside the VEFA framework? Is there a guarantee and who issued it? What is my recourse if delivery slips?
