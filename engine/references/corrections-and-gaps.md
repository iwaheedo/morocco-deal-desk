## Part 2 — What he left out

### Law that changed after he recorded
- **Cash now costs an extra 2%.** Since 1 July 2026 any part of a price above 300,000 MAD not paid by traceable bank means carries a 2% supplementary duty on the buyer (CGI art. 133-III). **H**
- **Preliminary contracts must be notarised.** Since 16 July 2026 (Law 41-25) a promise of sale or a property power of attorney must be an authentic deed, or it is void. Private "reservation" papers are worthless. **M**
- **Decennial building insurance** is compulsory only for buildings permitted from 30 Dec 2024. **H**

### The price is not the price
- **Never pay "under the table"** (*le noir*). Side agreements are void; penalties reach 100% of the tax evaded; the state can pre-empt the property within six months at the declared price; the undeclared part cannot be repatriated; and your declared cost — hence your taxable gain on resale — is distorted. **H**
- **Adverts are asking prices.** No reliable asking-to-sold statistic exists for Morocco; in 2026 sales volumes fell sharply while asking prices held. Ask agents what flats *sold* for.

### Getting your money back out
- Bring every dirham in **from abroad by bank transfer** into a convertible-dirham account and **keep the bank's foreign-currency certificates**. Only money documented this way can be freely taken out when you sell. **H**
- With a dirham mortgage, what you can repatriate on sale is your foreign-currency deposit + capital repaid from abroad + the gain. **H**
- A foreign non-resident can transfer rental income abroad however the purchase was financed; a Moroccan living abroad only if it was financed in foreign currency. Whether banks accept *Airbnb* income (no lease) for transfer is unverified. **H/L**
- Sterling is near a five-year high against the dirham (13.15; range about 11.7–13.2). Good for buying now; future rent converts back at whatever the rate is then. **H**

### Tax — entirely absent from the video
- **Long lets**: income tax on 60% of rent at the progressive scale (the old flat 10%/15% ended in 2022), plus 10.5% communal tax on the rent. About 12% of rent all-in at 84,000 MAD a year. **H**
- **Short lets**: unsettled. Practice: declared like ordinary rent. Tax office's written doctrine: a business (business tax, possible VAT). Central estimate **15% of bookings**, range 8–25%. Enforcement against undeclared Airbnb income has been stepped up since 2024. **L/M**
- **Selling**: 20% of the gain with a floor of **3% of the sale price**; the main-home exemption is lost once the flat has been let. The seller must lodge 5% of the price unless an advance ruling was obtained. **H**
- **UK residents** are taxed on the same income and gain at home, with credit for Moroccan income tax. For a 40% taxpayer this is usually the larger bill. **H**
- **Second home** you keep for yourself: housing tax with no abatement, roughly 0.6–1.2% of price a year. **M**

### Is short-letting even allowed?
- Law 80-14 requires an authorisation to run tourist accommodation, with fines of 50,000–500,000 MAD, and daily electronic declaration of guests. But no category fits an investor-owned flat, and the licensing rules for private lets were **still unpublished in Nov 2026**. The government said in Jan 2026 it is preparing a decree on buildings used for short lets. Widely practised, legally grey, likely to tighten. **H on the law, M on status**
- A building's own rules can restrict it. Read the *règlement de copropriété*. (No Moroccan case law found either way.) **L**
- Marrakech authorities ran control campaigns on furnished flats in 2025 (guest registration forms as in hotels). **M**

### Building and location risk
- **Earthquakes**: the Sept 2023 quake (Mw 6.8) damaged Marrakech's old medina; modern concrete districts were almost untouched. Seismic rules became mandatory in **2002** and were tightened in **2013–14**; enforcement was patchy. Agadir is in the highest hazard zone, Tangier and Marrakech next, Casablanca and Rabat lower. **H/M**
- **Hidden defects**: legal recourse covers collapse-type failures only, with a 30-day window to sue. **H**
- **Water**: severe stress in 2023–24 (hammam closures in Marrakech), relieved in 2026. Check the building's tank and pump. **H**
- **Oversupply**: listings are growing faster than visitors (Rabat listings +68% in a year; Marrakech revenue per listing flat to falling). **M**
- **The buyer inherits the seller's unpaid service charges** — get the syndic's clearance. **H**
- **Shared ownership**: buying a *share* of an undivided property lets co-owners pre-empt (chefaa). A separate apartment title is unaffected. **H**

### People
- **Estate agents are unregulated** — anyone can act as one. Never give an agent a deposit.
- **A developer's notary is not yours.** Choose your own, and check them in the Ordre des notaires directory.
- **Inheritance**: Moroccan family law applies to Moroccan nationals including dual nationals; a foreign will is not self-executing. Make a Moroccan-notarised will. **M**
- **Foreigners cannot buy agricultural land** outside the urban perimeter without a non-agricultural-use certificate (AVNA). Irrelevant to a city flat; decisive for a "villa with land". **M**
