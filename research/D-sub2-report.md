## 4. Construction quality and liability
- **Art. 769 of the Dahir des Obligations et Contrats**: "L'architecte ou ingénieur, et l'entrepreneur chargés directement par le maître sont responsables" if the work "s'écroule, en tout ou en partie, ou présente un danger évident de s'écrouler". Causes covered are materials, construction or soil defects. "Le délai de dix ans commence à courir du jour de la réception des travaux"; "L'action doit être intentée dans les trente jours" (https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/edocs/lexdocs/laws/fr/ma/ma064fr.pdf; La Vie Éco reproduction, https://www.lavieeco.com/?p=28820). H.
  - Conflict: the WIPO copy's first clause was read to me as "cinq années", La Vie Éco gives "10 années". I trust ten (matches the article's closing sentence and Law 59-13), but check the official text.
  - It covers collapse or threat of collapse only, and does not name the developer.
- **Mandatory insurance**: Law 59-13, with "deux arrêtés ministériels (3201-24 et 3202-24)" and "l'entrée en vigueur au 30 décembre 2024". Applies to "habitation de plus de trois étages ou de surface supérieure à 800 m²". A missing decennial certificate at the occupancy-permit request costs "6 dirhams/m²" (FNH, 28 Jun 2025, https://fnh.ma/articles/construction-nouvelles-assurances-obligatoires). H. Buildings permitted before 2025 were generally built without compulsory decennial cover (inference).
- **Off-plan law 107-12**: the seller must give a completion or refund guarantee, but "Deux décrets d'application sont toujours attendus" and "Les assureurs refusent de s'engager" (La Vie Éco, 10 Jun 2023, https://www.lavieeco.com/?p=301546). M; 2026 status NOT VERIFIED. Reservation contract "n'excédant pas six mois"; termination damages "entre 15 et 20% des montants versés" (FNH, 20 Jun 2021, https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/vefa-les-innovations-de-la-recente-loi-restent-contournables). ## Practical implications (my inference)
1. Earthquake damage in Marrakech tracked building type: prefer engineered reinforced-concrete blocks in Guéliz/Hivernage over médina or earthen stock, and avoid added storeys.
2. Age bands by permit date, not completion: pre-2002 has no mandatory seismic code; 2002 to about 2014 is RPS 2000 with three zones; later is the 2011 version; permits from late 2024 fall under Marrakech's revised values. Compliance was patchy, so ask for the structural study and inspection-office reports.
3. Agadir is in the top acceleration zone and Tangier is higher than Casablanca/Rabat; weight structural diligence accordingly.
4. Legal recourse for defects is narrow (collapse-type only, 30-day window) and decennial insurance is only compulsory from 30 Dec 2024. Ask for the policy certificate, and treat off-plan guarantees as weak.
5. Water risk is cyclical: 2026 is the best in a decade after 2024 lows. Check the building's tank and pump set-up and whether Safi water is actually flowing.
6. Demand is still growing but decelerating (+14% in 2025, +5% in 2026) while beds and listings grow faster. Underwrite flat or lower nightly rates and midweek gaps.
7. Airport traffic already exceeds stated capacity until the expansion lands (about 2028).
