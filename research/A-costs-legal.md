# A — Morocco residential purchase: acquisition costs, legal process, title, buyer protections

Research date: 18 November 2026. Scope: titled apartment bought by an individual (non-Moroccan foreigner or MRE).

**How to read this file**

- Every row gives: value, source, year the source reflects, a short verbatim quote (original language), confidence.
- "Primary" = text of the law/decree/official circular itself. "Secondary" = press, notary, law firm. "Blog" = commercial/marketing site (last resort).
- Access caveats:
  - The DGI's CGI 2026 and Note circulaire 737 were read in full, but from PDF copies mirrored on a private firm's site (upsilon-consulting.com); tax.gov.ma could not be reached directly. The documents are the DGI's own (title page "CODE GENERAL DES IMPOTS … 2026").
  - adala.justice.gov.ma, sgg.gov.ma and oc.gov.ma were unreachable from this environment. Law 32-09 (notaries) and the IGOC (exchange control) are therefore quoted only through secondary sources.
  - The web-search budget ran out before three checks were finished (agent commission split, permis d'habiter article, CAFPI simulator). They are listed in section 4.

**Did recent finance laws change these?**

- **LF 2025 (Law 60-24):** no change to the 4% / 5% / 3% rates. Art. 133-I-F footnotes cite only LF 2012, 2017, 2018 and 2024; the only LF 2025 footnote in art. 133 concerns leases over 10 years. Confidence: high (read from CGI 2026 footnotes).
- **LF 2026 (Law 50-25):** added the 2% supplementary duty (art. 133-III) and generalised the 200 MAD fixed duty on mortgage deeds for bank credit. Base rates unchanged. Confidence: high.
- **PLF 2027** (tabled 20 Oct 2026, not yet enacted on 18 Nov 2026): registration measures reported are a clarification on "actes prescrits" and "Généralisation de l'attestation d'enregistrement des actes." No change to home-purchase rates reported. Source: Challenge/MAP, 24 Oct 2026 — https://www.challenge.ma/plf-2027-principales-mesures-fiscales-proposees-325920/. Confidence: medium (press summary of the presentation note; bill can still be amended).

**Is the mortgage tariff marginal (by slice) or flat?** Two readings circulate:

- By slice (trusted): a notary's column gives a worked example — a 5.5 M loan costs 1,250 + 71,250 + 2,500 = 75,000. FNH, 20 Nov 2019 — https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-financiere-maroc/transactions-immobilieres-ce-qu-il-faut-savoir. Quote: "0,5% sur la tranche de 0 à 250.000 dirhams faisant l'objet de l'hypothèque". Bladi (9 Jun 2026) uses the same "tranche" wording.
- Flat 1.5% of the whole loan: used by most online simulators and by the video's 8.4%.
- I trust the slice reading because it comes from a practising notary with arithmetic shown, and matches the decree's wording. Confidence: medium-high. The difference on a 1.05 M loan is 2,500 MAD.

Sources claiming the sale registration is "1% + 200 MAD" (LesMRE older text, Wafir) conflict with the decree and are wrong: 1% applies to first registration (réquisition), not to a sale.

#### a.6 Summary percentages to use

- Registration duty: **4.00%**
- Land registry: **1.50%** + 100 MAD
- Notary: **~1.00%** + 20% VAT = **~1.20%**
- Fixed items: ~0.1–0.2% on a 1.5 M purchase
- **Cash total ≈ 6.8%** (budget 7%)
- Mortgage adds **≈1.3% of the loan amount** (≈0.9% of price at 70% LTV) → **≈7.8%** (budget 8%)
- Untraceable payment: **+2%** — avoid by paying only by bank transfer or cheque through the notary.

**One notary or two? Who chooses?**

- Finding: a single notary normally handles the sale and advises both sides. The buyer chooses, because the buyer pays. ReaConsult (30 May 2026, blog): "l'acquéreur choisit son notaire"; parties may agree on a common notary. Aboulachbal describes the notary as "un tiers de confiance" who is there to "conseiller les parties". Confidence: medium.
- On new-builds the developer's notary is usually used (FNH interview: "certains promoteurs immobiliers se dirigent systématiquement vers un seul notaire"). Confidence: medium.
- Whether a second notary can co-act and how fees are split: **NOT VERIFIED**.

**Can a lawyer be engaged, and is it advisable?**

- Until 15 July 2026, a lawyer admitted before the Cour de cassation could draft property deeds as an alternative to a notary or adoul (Law 39-08 art. 4, BO 7158: "dans un acte authentique ou dans un acte à date certaine, rédigé par un avocat agréé près la Cour de cassation").
- **Law 41-25** (promulgated 3 July 2026, BO n° 7526 of 16 July 2026) removed that option: sale, promise of sale and special power of attorney must be authentic deeds, on pain of nullity. Source: Bladi, 10 Aug 2026 — https://www.bladi.net/mre-juillet-vente-immobiliere-maroc-nulle,123032.html. Quote: "Vente, promesse de vente ou procuration spéciale doivent désormais être établies par acte authentique." Adoption confirmed by La Vie éco, 16 Jun 2026 — https://www.lavieeco.com/?p=454928. Confidence: medium-high (two press sources; BO text not read).
- A lawyer can still be retained purely as the buyer's adviser. That is a judgement call, not a sourced rule: it makes sense where the notary was chosen by the seller or developer, for off-plan, or for anything not cleanly titled.

- Launched Feb 2019. Alerts the owner by email/SMS of any entry on the title (mortgage, seizure, sale). Open to owners in Morocco or abroad. Under decree 2.18.181 every registration request enrols the beneficiary, who must give a phone number and email.
- Source: Hespress, 13 Feb 2019 — https://fr.hespress.com/48993-48993.html. Quote: "tout propriétaire adhérant à cette plateforme, au Maroc ou à l'Etranger". Confidence: medium-high.

**Art. 618-6 — maximum schedule (verbatim lines)**

- "-5% à la conclusion du contrat de réservation ;"
- "-5% à la conclusion du contrat préliminaire ou 10% en cas de non existence du contrat de réservation ;"
- "-10% au lancement des travaux ;"
- "-60% divisé en trois phases à payer, selon l'accord des parties, à l'achèvement des travaux de réalisation de chaque phase ;" (foundations to ground floor; structural works; finishing works)
- "-20% à la conclusion du contrat de vente définitif et la remise des clés."

Conflicting versions seen: Gide (Apr 2016, on the bill) gave 5/75/20; ReaConsult gives 20/30/30/20. The statute text above is the one to trust.

**Is the video's pattern (5,000 MAD + 20% / 20% / 30% / 30%) consistent with the law?**

- Cumulative legal ceiling: 10% once the preliminary contract is signed; 20% at start of works; 40% / 60% / 80% as the three phases complete; 100% at keys.
- His first 20% is above the ceiling unless works have already started. His later tranches (40%, 70%) track roughly at or under the ceiling if tied to real milestones, and his 30% final tranche is more protective than the legal 20%.
- So: not the statutory schedule. It looks like a developer's own "réservation" plan outside the VEFA framework.
- "Lose your money if you pay late" is not what the statute says: the cap is 1%/month, 10%/year, and termination costs 15–20% of sums paid.

**Buyer checklist for off-plan** (Bladi, 21 Jul 2026 — https://www.bladi.net/mre-pieges-eviter-acheter-logement-plan-maroc,122634.html, plus the statute):

- Land title in the developer's name, with encumbrances listed (order your own certificat de propriété).
- Building permit number and date — "aucun contrat de réservation ou contrat préliminaire ne doit être conclu avant l'obtention du permis de construire."

- Your own notary or adviser, not only the developer's.

- If the mortgage is charged at a flat 1.5% of the loan (15,750 + 100): total 118,850 = **7.92%**.
- Mortgage-specific cost ≈ 13,850 = 1.32% of the loan.
- Not included (not verified): any separate notary fee for the loan deed, bank file fee, valuation, borrower insurance. If the bank registers the mortgage for more than the principal, the ANCFCC fee rises accordingly.
- Later cost: mortgage release 500 MAD plus the notary's fee.

### 3.3 Reconciling with the video's 8.4%

At 100% loan on a 1,250,000 MAD (≈ £100k) purchase, with a flat 1.5% mortgage fee and 1% + 20% VAT notary fee: 4% + 1.5% + 1.5% + 1.2% + ~0.15% fixed ≈ **8.35%**. So 8.4% describes a fully financed purchase. It overstates a 70% loan by about 0.5 points and a cash purchase by about 1.5 points.

### 3.4 What would add to these figures

- Any cash or undocumented payment above the threshold: +2% of that part (30,000 MAD on the full price).
- Agent commission if the buyer pays one: 2.5% = 37,500 MAD (plus VAT if charged; rate not verified).
- Translation, POA legalisation/exequatur, independent lawyer.
